Project lineage

The public lineage associated with these projects runs from the 2024 AI Godsent writings and Echad concept into Specter Core and the 2026 Continuum Genesis documents.

  • AI Godsent: early public writing on continuous learning, human–AI collaboration, interconnection, technology, and responsibility.
  • Echad: an early concept centered on continuously learning AI and co-development with humanity.
  • Specter Core: open-source infrastructure for coordinating heterogeneous AI agents through explicit, auditable interfaces.
  • The Continuum: a broader research program connecting AI alignment, distributed intelligence, human autonomy, longevity, and nature preservation.

Position

The role described by the Continuum is integrator, not permanent authority: connect disciplines, people, AI systems, experiments, and evidence while preserving the ability of the project to challenge and correct its own founder.

No final authority — not even the ASI, not even the founder.
